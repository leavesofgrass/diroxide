# Keyboard: the Explorer and Finder preset

This page is generated by `cargo xtask keyboard` from `crates/diroxide-app/keys/presets/explorer-finder.toml`. Do not edit it by hand; change the preset file instead.

For people who know Windows Explorer or the Finder: Backspace goes back, Alt+P previews, and on macOS the Finder's Command keys.

Choose it in the Keys list (its first row, Choose a preset), with Key presets in the palette or in the Tools menu's Keys submenu, or from a shell with `diro keys preset explorer-finder`. Your own keys in `keymap.toml` stay on top of it, and every key it does not list is the one on [the keyboard page](keyboard.md). Each line below gives the key first, then the command, the key it replaces, and the command's id.

Single letters still jump to names with this preset.

Its keys come from Microsoft's keyboard shortcuts page for File Explorer and Apple's Mac keyboard shortcuts page for the Finder.

## In a terminal

### Everywhere

- Alt+P or Shift+F3: Quick view. Was Shift+F3. (view.quick-view)
- Alt+U: Plugins menu. Was Alt+P. (menu.plugins)

### In a panel

- Alt+Left or Backspace: Back. Was Alt+Left. (go.back)
- Left or Alt+Up: Up one folder. Was Left or Backspace or Alt+Up. (panel.up)

## In the window

### Everywhere

- Alt+P or Shift+F3: Quick view. Was Shift+F3. (view.quick-view)
- Alt+U: Plugins menu. Was Alt+P. (menu.plugins)

### In a panel

- Alt+Left or Backspace: Back. Was Alt+Left. (go.back)
- Left or Alt+Up: Up one folder. Was Left or Backspace or Alt+Up. (panel.up)
- Ctrl+A: Select all. Was Ctrl+A or Keypad Plus. (select.all)
- Ctrl+Shift+A: Select none. Was Ctrl+Shift+A or Keypad Minus. (select.none)
- *: Invert selection. Was * or Keypad Star. (select.invert)
- Plus: Select by pattern. Was Plus or Keypad Slash. (select.pattern)

## On macOS, in the window

On macOS the window has these keys as well, the Finder's.

### Everywhere

- Shift+F3 or Cmd+Y: Quick view. Was Shift+F3. (view.quick-view)

### In a panel

- F8 or Delete or Cmd+Backspace: Move to trash. Was F8 or Delete. (file.trash)
- Cmd+L or Option+D or Cmd+Enter or Cmd+Shift+G: Go to a path. Was Cmd+L or Option+D or Cmd+Enter. (go.path)
- Option+Left or Cmd+[: Back. Was Option+Left. (go.back)
- Option+Right or Cmd+]: Forward. Was Option+Right. (go.forward)
- Left or Backspace or Cmd+Up: Up one folder. Was Left or Backspace or Option+Up. (panel.up)
- Option+Home or Cmd+Shift+H: Home folder. Was Option+Home. (go.home)
- Option+Enter or Cmd+I: Details. Was Option+Enter. (file.details)
- Option+Period or Cmd+Shift+Period: Show hidden files. Was Option+Period. (view.hidden)
