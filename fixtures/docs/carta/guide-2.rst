Archives
========

Opening one
-----------

#. Move to the archive.
#. Press Enter.

Formats
-------

+--------+-------+--------+
| Format | Read  | Write  |
+========+=======+========+
| zip    | yes   | yes    |
+--------+-------+--------+
| 7z     | yes   | yes    |
+--------+-------+--------+

Packing
-------

* Mark the files.
* Choose Pack.

  * Pick the format.
  * Pick the level.

The `archive guide <https://example.org/archives>`_ and the
`format list <https://example.org/formats>`_ have the details.
